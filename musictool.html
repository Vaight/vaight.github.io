MUSIC CATALOG TOOL

This tool is similar to discogs or other music collecting tools. The idea here is to catalog what music you have PHYSICALLY collected so that you can share this with other people or just keep track of what you own.

Below you will see a list with two tabs. You can edit the "collection" tab to be what you currently own. Now there is also the "wishlist" tab. This is for if you are going to share this list with people who may be buying you gifts.
