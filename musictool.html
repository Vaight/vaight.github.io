What is this tool for?

This tool will allow you to list what music you have collected so far. You can send this list to friends / family so they can see what you have. This is not a search tool, just a tool for listing your collection. This tool works for CDs, vinyls, tapes, or any other mediums of music you have collected. All you need to do is press the plus (+) button to add something to your collection.

The website should load your collection automatically, but if it doesn't, be sure to let me know.

Once you have finished your collection or are ready to save it so far, press the save button on the top of the screen.
